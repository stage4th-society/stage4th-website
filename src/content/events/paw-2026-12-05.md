playwrightZh: "W. W. Jacobs（1863—1943），全名 William Wymark Jacobs，是英国短篇小说家与小说家。他早年曾在邮政储蓄银行担任文员，后于1899年成为全职作家。成长于伦敦泰晤士河码头附近的经历，为他的创作提供了丰富素材；他的作品常描绘水手、码头工人与普通人的日常生活，并以写实笔触、温和幽默和精巧的情节见长。代表作品包括《众多货物》、《海胆》、《轻载货物》和《驳船上的女士及其他故事》等。发表于1902年的《猴爪》是他最负盛名的作品之一，以愿望、命运及其代价为主题，至今仍被视为超自然恐怖短篇小说中的经典。"
playwrightEn: "W. W. Jacobs (1863–1943), born William Wymark Jacobs, was an English short-story writer and novelist. He worked as a clerk at the Post Office Savings Bank before becoming a full-time writer in 1899. His experiences growing up near the docks of the River Thames in London provided rich material for his fiction, which frequently portrays sailors, dockworkers, and the everyday lives of working people with realism, gentle humour, and carefully crafted plots. His notable works include Many Cargoes, Sea Urchins, Light Freights, and The Lady of the Barge and Other Stories. Published in 1902, The Monkey’s Paw remains his best-known work and an enduring classic of supernatural fiction, exploring the dangerous consequences of desire and the human impulse to challenge fate."
