descriptionZh: "舞台朗读系列带着两部全新的短剧再次回归。两部作品共同讲述那些我们被期待去过的人生，以及想象另一种可能所需要的勇气。\n\nJ. M. Barrie 创作于 1910 年的《十二英镑》，由 Calandya Zhang 进行本土化改编并执导。故事从一个看似成功而体面的家庭展开，一位曾经为了独立而离开安稳生活的女性意外归来，也打破了原有的平静。此次改编将故事置于新的文化背景之中，同时保留作品关于婚姻、独立与成功定义的核心追问。\n\nGeorge Middleton 创作于 1913 年的《传统》，由 Wendy Chi 执导。作品以克制而细腻的方式，让家庭责任、个人理想、世代期待与自由选择之间的矛盾逐渐浮现，也让不同世代不得不重新面对彼此所作出的选择。\n\n两部作品在风格上各有不同，却在精神上彼此呼应。它们邀请我们重新思考：谁在定义成功？传统如何影响我们的决定？而选择属于自己的人生，究竟意味着什么？\n\n这两部经典作品也将继续 Stage 4th Society 对旧作当代意义的探索，让来自过去的声音，在今天重新与我们相遇。"
descriptionEn: "Stage 4th Society’s staged-reading series returns with a new pairing of short plays, bringing together two stories about the lives we are expected to lead and the courage it takes to imagine another path.\n\nJ. M. Barrie’s The Twelve-Pound Look (1910), localized, adapted, and directed by Calandya Zhang, follows a seemingly successful household unsettled by the unexpected return of a woman who once chose independence over comfort. Reimagined in a new cultural setting, the play retains its central questions about marriage, independence, and the meaning of success.\n\nGeorge Middleton's Tradition (1913), directed by Wendy Chi Wen-Chieh, brings questions of family, duty, ambition, and personal freedom quietly to the surface as one generation confronts the choices of another.\n\nDifferent in tone but closely connected in spirit, the two plays invite us to consider who defines success, how tradition shapes our decisions, and what it means to choose a life of one’s own.\n\nTogether, these works continue Stage 4th Society’s exploration of classic plays that still speak directly to the present."
playwrightZh: "J. M. Barrie 是苏格兰剧作家与小说家，以创作《彼得·潘》闻名于世。他的剧作常以细腻的笔触探讨童年与成年的对立、幻想与现实的边界，代表作品包括《彼得·潘》、《可敬的克莱顿》、《人人皆知的女人》、《十二英镑》和《亲爱的布鲁图斯》等，其中《十二英镑》以讽刺的视角审视婚姻与女性独立。\n\nGeorge Middleton 是美国剧作家、导演与制作人，也是美国剧作家协会的重要推动者之一，曾于1927年至1929年担任该协会主席。他创作了约二十部剧作，以独幕剧见长，代表作包括与 Guy Bolton 合作的《Polly With a Past》和《Adam and Eva》，以及探讨家庭、传统与个人自由之间张力的独幕剧《传统》。"
playwrightEn: "J. M. Barrie was a Scottish playwright and novelist, best known as the creator of Peter Pan. His plays often explore the tension between childhood and adulthood, fantasy and reality, with works including Peter Pan, The Admirable Crichton, What Every Woman Knows, The Twelve-Pound Look, and Dear Brutus. The Twelve-Pound Look offers a satirical examination of marriage and women's independence.\n\nGeorge Middleton was an American playwright, director, and producer, and a key figure in the Dramatists Guild of America, serving as its president from 1927 to 1929. He wrote some twenty produced plays and was a known proponent of the one-act form. His best-known works include Polly With a Past and Adam and Eva (co-written with Guy Bolton), and Tradition, a one-act play that examines the tensions between family, tradition, and personal freedom."
